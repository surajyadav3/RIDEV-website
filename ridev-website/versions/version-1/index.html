Under the bonnet

Free maintenance only works if you can measure it.

“Included maintenance” is easy to promise and expensive to keep. RIDEV runs its own fleet software so every vehicle, every repair job, every swap and every rupee of spare-part cost is tracked to a registration number — across all eight hubs, in real time.

One console for the whole fleet

Every vehicle carries a live status — with a rider, in repair, at an OEM service centre, on an insurance claim, or ready to allocate. Nothing sits in a spreadsheet.

A partner network that quotes before it works

Maintenance partners raise a parts estimate against the vehicle, RIDEV approves it, the job is done, the invoice is attached and the vehicle is marked ready. Warranty and insurance-covered parts are separated at line-item level.

Swaps logged, not improvised

Vehicle swaps are recorded against the rider's booking, so a replacement bike takes minutes and the subscription simply continues. No new paperwork, no lost day.

Built in-house, on purpose

The console, the maintenance partner portal and the swap log are RIDEV's own software, not off-the-shelf fleet tools bent into shape. That is what makes an all-inclusive weekly rent something we can actually price and keep.

    Questions riders ask

    The straight answers.

    Is the battery swap really free?

    Yes. Battery swapping is included in the weekly rent with no cap on how many times you swap. It is a deliberate choice: energy is the single most unpredictable cost for a delivery rider, and making it free is what turns a rental into a genuinely fixed weekly number.

    What if the bike breaks down in the middle of a shift?

    Bring it to your hub — or call and we will arrange recovery. Repairs, servicing and spare parts are covered by RIDEV. If the vehicle needs to stay back, you leave on a replacement bike the same day and your subscription simply carries over to it.

    Do I need a down payment, a loan or a credit check?

    None of the three. You pay the first week's rent and a refundable security deposit. There is no financing, no EMI and no ownership risk — if you stop riding, you stop paying.

    What documents do I need?

    A valid driving licence, Aadhaar and a working phone number. KYC is completed at the hub, usually within minutes of you walking in.

    Which bike should I take?

    It depends on your daily distance. If you cover long routes, the higher-range models — the Ather Rizta at 125 km or the TVS iQube at 105 km — cost more per week but need fewer swap stops. If you ride dense city routes with a hub nearby, the entry models at ₹1,400 a week are usually the better earner.

    Can I use a RIDEV bike for Zomato, Swiggy, Blinkit, Zepto or Delhivery work?

    Yes — that is exactly what the fleet is built for. A large share of RIDEV vehicles are configured specifically for delivery duty, and RIDEV works directly with logistics operators including Delhivery on dedicated fleet deployments.

    Which cities can I rent in?

    Delhi, Gurugram, Hyderabad, Chennai and Mumbai are live today, from eight hubs. Pune and Bengaluru are provisioned on the platform and will open as vehicles are allocated.

    Is insurance included?

    Yes. The vehicle carries insurance throughout your subscription, and RIDEV manages the claim process. Insurance-related repairs are handled inside the same maintenance workflow as everything else.
